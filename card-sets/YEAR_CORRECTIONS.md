# Year corrections in hitster-de-aaaa0075.csv

The file comes from the open pull request #57 of [andygruber/songseeker-hitster-playlists](https://github.com/andygruber/songseeker-hitster-playlists). Where the same song (same title and artist) is in another card set of this folder, the year of the other set was taken, and only the Year column was changed. This list is what changed (76 cards). The original file is in the history of this repository.

## Second pass: checked against the physical cards

99 cards were read with the reader mode of the Android app and compared with the printed cards. The file now shows what the card shows (a card-set file describes the cards, not a streaming catalogue):

- **Year:** the year printed on the card (35 cards changed). Three of them differ from the first pass, which took the year of another set: card 208 (1997, was 1998), card 192 (1992, was 1993) and card 15 (1993, was 1992). The printed card wins.
- **Title:** the title as printed. Catalogue additions such as "- Single Version", "- 1990 Remaster", "- Radio Edit" or "(feat. ...)" are removed (59 cards).
- **Artist:** the artist line as printed, "A & B" or "A (feat. B)" instead of "A - B" (48 cards).

The columns URL, Hashed Info and Youtube-Title were not touched.
